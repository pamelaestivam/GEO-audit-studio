# Strategy review, 2026-11-14: free engines, and what to do next

Asked by Ricardo: analyse how to evolve the product, and evaluate a list of free
model providers suggested by another session. **Analysis and plan only. Nothing in
the product was changed.**

**Status: agreed by the owner side on 2026-11-14** ("I agree with your recommendation").
The decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (same date); the build specification is
`docs/PLAN_2026-11.md`; the engineering backlog is `docs/ENGINEERING_BACKLOG.md`.

Confidence words are the ones `docs/INSIGHTS.md` uses: **MEASURED** (run and seen),
**READ** (read on the vendor's own page or in this repo's code, today), **AGENT**
(a search snippet or third-party article, not the vendor's page), **INFERRED**
(reasoned, not observed).

Two consequences worth saying plainly:

- Part of this repository's long fight with "quota exhausted" (the `TECH_DEBT.md` 2.3
  family) may have been this: an allowance of zero reads exactly like a used-up
  allowance. INFERRED, unproven; one real call settles it.
- An answer that comes back **without** any search (no searches run, no sources) is
  accepted today as a normal answer and scored. If Google ever answers instead of
  refusing (for example after a silent re-route), the report would present a
  from-memory answer as a web-searched one. READ (`collectGeminiEvidence` keeps
  whatever came back; nothing looks at whether a search ran).

## 2. The suggested free providers, judged for THIS product

The question that sorts the list: **what job would the model do here?**

- **Job A, answer engine (the thing we measure and show a client).** It must search
  the live web, return its sources, and be something a client recognises as an AI
  their customers use. A plain model with no search is not this.
- **Job B, memory probe.** What a model says about a brand from training alone, no
  search. A real signal, but a different claim, and it must never share a number
  with Job A.
- **Job C, writer.** The back-office call that writes the qualitative analysis from
  evidence already collected. Any capable model can do it.

Almost everything on the list is a host for open models with no search, so it can
only do B or C. That is the main reason the list is weaker than it looks.

| Option | What was found today | A: answer engine | C: writer | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini API** | Free search only on `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (and `2.5-flash` until 2026-12-01), 500 a day shared. Free-tier request limits are no longer printed; they show only in AI Studio. Free-tier content is "used to improve our products" (READ) | The only one at $0, on an old small model | Yes, without search | **Fix first** (section 4, step 1) |
| **Groq** | Its searching system, Compound, was decommissioned 2026-09-21; the Llama models are gone from it. What remains: `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, `gpt-oss-20b`, `qwen`, at 30 requests a minute, 1,000 a day, **8,000 tokens a minute** (the docs page does not make clear whether that table is the free plan). `gpt-oss` has a built-in `browser_search`; whether it is free is not stated (READ, console.groq.com/docs) | Unproven; needs a 30-minute trial | Best free candidate, but 8,000 tokens a minute is tight for a three-question analysis | **Trial** |

Three things the roundups do not say:

1. **Free is shrinking everywhere, and fast.** In four months: Google removed free
   search from its current models, Groq retired its searching system, Mistral
   renamed its free plan and dropped its monthly credit, GitHub Models reportedly
   closed. Any design that depends on one free tier staying as it is will break
   without warning. The product needs to notice such a change itself (section 4,
   step 2), whichever providers it uses.
2. **A free model with no search is not an "AI engine" a client knows.** Showing
   "Qwen" or "gpt-oss" beside Gemini as if they were peers would be the kind of
   presentation `CLAUDE.md` forbids: a number that looks like a measurement of the
   AI their customers use, and is not.
3. **Even the real engines' APIs are not the apps.** One vendor study (Surfer,
   13,779 answers; a company that sells the alternative, so directional only)
   found that brands named through an API matched what the app showed only about
   a quarter of the time. Paid competitors mostly read the apps' screens instead.
   Our report should say "Gemini through its API, with Google Search", never just
   "Gemini says". AGENT.

## 3. Where the product stands, honestly

- **Engineering is far ahead of proof.** Over two thousand checks, resumable
  audits, a shared database, Google sign-in; and no real audit read by a person,
  no real client, no market section in the PRD.
- **The market has moved to paid, multi-engine, daily tracking.** Otterly from
  about $29 a month (15 questions, four engines), Peec from about EUR 89, Profound
  by quote; one competitor (AthenaHQ) has a standing free plan (AGENT, several
  comparison pages of November 2026; none opened at the vendor). A $0 single-engine
  tracker cannot win that race, and should not try.
- **What this product has that a tracker does not:** it shows the evidence (the
  verbatim answer, the sources, "n of M", what was not counted and why) and refuses
  to invent. That is what a consultant needs to put in front of a client, and it
  is cheap to run at consultant volume: 500 searched answers a day is far more
  than one person's audits.

**Recommended position: the audit instrument of a consultant or small agency,
used first by the owner herself on real businesses.** Not a self-serve tracker
yet. This changes the PRD's "ICP deferred" to a concrete first user without
closing any later door.

## 4. Plan, in order

Each step names who does it. Nothing here needs money.

**Step 0. Find out what is true (owner, about 15 minutes).**
1. Open `https://aistudio.google.com`, the rate-limit page for the key's project.
   Read the line for search grounding. Expected: 0 for Gemini 3, a number for
   Gemini 2.5. Note the requests-per-day for `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`.
2. In Vercel, project `geo-audit-studio`, Settings, Environment Variables,
   Production: add `GEMINI_MODEL` = `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`. Redeploy once.
3. Sign in to the live site, run one audit with one question, and send the
   assistant a screenshot of the result and of the answer text.
Know first: on this older model "Auto-Detect" and "Generate Query Matrix" may stop
working (they ask for a search and a structured reply in one request, which older
models are believed to refuse; unverified). They probably do not work today either
if search is refused. Typing the details yourself always works.
Why only the owner: the workspace holds no key and cannot open Vercel or AI Studio.
This is `docs/PROGRAM.md` O-7. It changes which model the report names; under
decision G the owner is told before it happens, and this is that notice.

**Step 1. Make this failure impossible to miss (assistant, one small PR).**
- A refused search gets its own sentence and state where it can be told apart
  from a used-up allowance, and never trips the come-back-at-midnight breaker.
  (The exact design is `docs/PLAN_2026-11.md` P2; the first sketch, an extra call
  inside every failing audit, was dropped after review because it would have
  changed call counts that existing tests pin.)
- The readiness check asks the same kind of question an audit asks (with search),
  so a green check means audits can run.
- Every answer records whether a search actually ran. An answer with no search is
  shown as "answered from memory, not counted" (or failed), never scored as a
  searched answer.
- Proved the usual way: the built server against a fake Gemini that refuses
  searched calls and accepts plain ones.

**Step 2. Let the daily check see real audits (owner, 5 minutes).** Add the two
repository secrets in `docs/PROGRAM.md` O-3 step 4. Until then the daily check
cannot tell a working product from a broken one.

**Step 3. The first real audits (owner leads, assistant reads).** Five real
businesses the owner knows. For each: read the report against the raw answers
(this is PROGRAM row 10, blocked since October), save the answers as test
fixtures, and write down what the business owner asked for that the report did
not give. This is the missing market evidence, and it costs nothing.

**Step 4. A second voice, only if it passes a trial (assistant; owner makes two
free keys).** A manually started GitHub workflow (runners can reach any host; the
workspace cannot) asks the same few questions of Mistral with `web_search` and
Groq `gpt-oss-120b` with `browser_search`, and saves what comes back. An engine is
adopted only if, on a free key, it searches, returns sources, and names
businesses. In the same change the fixed list of four engines becomes a small
registry where each engine states what it is (searches or not, free or paid, the
exact model), so the report can label it truthfully. At most one engine is added
from this step.

**Step 5. Cut the analysis call loose from Gemini (assistant).** Today every
audit needs Gemini twice over: to answer and to write. Make the written analysis
a separate, optional step that any free writer can do (Groq first, Gemini without
search as fallback), and let the measured numbers stand without it. This removes
the "Gemini is mandatory" debt (`TECH_DEBT.md` 1.2, 3.4) and takes one Gemini
request off every audit (the analysis call carries no search, so the 500-a-day
search allowance is not what it saves).

**Step 6. Give a reason to come back (assistant).** In this order: run the same
audit again and show what changed (history now survives); a report that can be
forwarded (print or PDF); the source-gap worklist; several runs of the same
question with the spread shown, now that 500 a day leaves room for it.

**What to stop or hold:** more reliability plumbing (slices S6 and S7 of the
step-wise audit), until step 3 has produced real audits. They protect a product
that has not yet measured anything real.

## 5. Decisions for the owner, with a recommendation for each

| # | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep $0? | **Yes, for now.** Lift it only when step 3 has five real audits and at least one business asks for more. Then the first money is a hard-capped small monthly sum on Google's paid tier, which includes 5,000 searched requests a month on current models before any search fee (READ); token charges still apply and a billing account ends the "cannot be charged" guarantee, so a spending cap must be confirmed first. |
| 2 | Switch the live site to `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` now? | **Yes** (step 0). It is the only free searching model. The report will name it honestly. |
| 3 | Who is the first user? | **The owner, as a consultant, on real businesses.** |
| 4 | Show open models with no search as extra "engines"? | **No.** At most, later, one clearly separate "what models remember about you" section, never blended into visibility. |
| 5 | Meta Muse Spark? | **Not now.** Paid, US-only, waitlisted. Revisit with decision 1. |
| 6 | Where are the owner and her clients based? | Needed for two checks: Meta is US-only, and Google's terms for the free tier may not allow serving people in the EU, UK or Switzerland (from memory, **not checked today**). If Europe: check that clause before any client uses the site. |

## 6. What this review did not verify

- Any real call to any engine (no keys here).
- That the forum repliers speak for Google.
- Groq's and Mistral's free limits and whether their search tools are free.
- Meta's current prices, the competitor prices, the chatbot market shares (all
  third-party pages).
- Whether `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` will stay; it is the last of its generation,
  so assume it will not, and treat step 4 as the hedge.
